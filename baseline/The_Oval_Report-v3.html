League games only, to 7 November 2026. Momentum uses a 0–100 index: 50 neutral, higher rising, lower falling. The most recent three results contribute about 69% of the weighted signal. Wins always add momentum, regardless of league position; draws add momentum only against stronger opponents. HONEST LIMITATIONS

This is a retrospective walk-forward test, not a record of predictions published before kick-off. The model settings are held fixed across all games and no later results enter earlier predictions. On the opening day, all clubs begin with a neutral prior because previous-season ratings are not part of this model. The 2026/27 match results were compiled after the games were played; the accuracy score was specified before examining the backtest output. Neither the model nor the rubric was tuned after the audit. Because the model was originally configured in November, this is not an independent prospective validation of parameters chosen before the season.

04 / Season arithmetic

THE MATHS.

No arbitrary targets: these are points thresholds derived from the final standings in the 5,000 simulated seasons. They update whenever you fix a match result.

Each target is the simulated points total of the club in that finishing position, with a central 80% range. This is a probabilistic benchmark, not a guaranteed threshold: ties and goal difference matter. Second is the direct European league route; lower places may require cup outcomes or a separate play-off.

Method: 5,000 repeat simulations. Match goal rates blend league goals, points per match and exponentially weighted last-five league form, regressed towards league averages, with random variation in team strength. Each club completes 33 matches before a simulated top-six/bottom-six split and five final fixtures. All 111 published remaining pre-split pairings, including a postponed fixture with its date still pending, are used directly. These are model estimates, not bookmaker odds, and exclude cup qualification routes.
